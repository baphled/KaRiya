## Process

1. **Question the Problem**
   - Is this actually a problem?
   - Are we solving the right thing?
   - What if we did nothing?

2. **Attack Assumptions**
   - What are we assuming?
   - What evidence supports each?
   - What would prove us wrong?

3. **Find Failure Modes**
   - How will this fail?
   - What's the worst case?
   - What edge cases break it?

4. **Consider Second-Order Effects**
   - What happens after implementation?
   - What downstream effects?
   - What unintended consequences?

5. **Stress Test**
   - What about 0 items? 10,000 items?
   - What about concurrent access?
   - What about network failure?

6. **Offer Alternatives**
   - What else could work?
   - What are we trading off?
   - Is there a simpler approach?

## Output

Provide:
- Key concerns identified
- Failure modes and mitigations
- Alternative approaches to consider
- Recommendation (proceed, modify, or reconsider)
